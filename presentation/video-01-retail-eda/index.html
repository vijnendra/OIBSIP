Business Recommendations

Strategy
Recommendation 01
Boost Q3 with targeted promotions
Insight: Q3 generated only $96,045 (lowest), with September at just $23,620.
Impact: Seasonal slump creates a revenue gap of ~$30K vs strongest quarter.
Action: Launch mid-summer sales events and back-to-school campaigns to lift Q3 revenue.
Recommendation 02
Maximize the Electronics advantage
Insight: Electronics leads revenue at $156,905 despite fewer transactions than Clothing.
Impact: Higher unit prices ($25–$500 range) drive disproportionate revenue per transaction.
Action: Expand electronics inventory, bundle products, and promote higher-margin electronics during peak months.
Recommendation 03
Loyalty programs for the 46–55 age group
Insight: The 46–55 age group generates the highest revenue at $100,690.
Impact: This segment outspends younger demographics by up to 25%.
Action: Implement exclusive loyalty rewards and personalized offers targeting this high-value segment.
Recommendation 04
Gender-balanced marketing strategy
Insight: Female customers contribute $232,840 (51%) vs Male $223,160 (49%).
Impact: Near-equal gender split suggests broad market appeal.
Action: Maintain gender-neutral core campaigns while creating segment-specific cross-sell opportunities in Beauty (Female) and Electronics (Male).
