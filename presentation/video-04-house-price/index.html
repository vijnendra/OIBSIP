The Problem

Real estate pricing is driven by complex combinations of location, size, and neighborhood wealth. The goal is to build an algorithm capable of estimating property values based on these attributes.
🎯

The Objective

Train a multivariate Linear Regression model on the California Housing dataset to predict the median_house_value for a given district block.
