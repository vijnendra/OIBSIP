Strategic Finding

🎯

Developer Recommendations

Strategy
01. Choose the Right Niche
Avoid highly saturated markets
Finding: Family, Games, and Tools are flooded.
Action: Target high-sentiment niches like Health & Fitness or Education where users are more forgiving and engaged, offering easier organic discovery.
02. Monetization Strategy
Embrace the Freemium model
Finding: 92.6% of successful apps are free.
Action: Do not rely on upfront paid downloads. Offer a high-quality free core experience and monetize through in-app purchases or ads.
03. Optimize App Size
Keep initial downloads lightweight
Finding: The apps with the highest install counts are generally optimized for size.
Action: Keep the initial APK/bundle size low to reduce download friction, utilizing dynamic delivery for extra assets.
