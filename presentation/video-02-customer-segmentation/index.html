Why Log Transform?

RFM features are heavily right-skewed. A customer with £280,206 spend vs the median of £668 would dominate the clustering. Log transformation compresses extreme values while preserving relative order.
⚖️

Why StandardScaler?

K-Means uses Euclidean distance. Without scaling, features with larger numeric ranges (Monetary: 3–280,206) would dominate over smaller ranges (Frequency: 1–209). Standardization gives each feature equal influence.

Key Strategic Insights

Insights
High-Value Customers
13.2% of customers drive disproportionate revenue
571 Champions average £9,823 in total spend — nearly 5× the overall mean of £2,048. These customers are the revenue backbone and should receive VIP treatment.
Retention Risk
21.6% of customers are inactive (259+ days)
938 customers haven't purchased in over 8 months. One targeted win-back campaign could recover significant revenue, but budget should be capped to avoid wasteful spending.
Growth Potential
65.2% are new/promising — the largest segment
2,829 customers show recent activity but low frequency (2.6 orders avg). Converting even 10% to repeat buyers would significantly increase revenue.
Spend Distribution
Massive variance: £3.75 to £280,206 total spend
The median spend of £668 vs mean of £2,048 reveals extreme right skew. One-size-fits-all marketing wastes budget. Segment-specific campaigns are essential.
